# GearUp: Wholesale (B2B) Terms for Gyms & Studios

*Sample knowledge-base document for the RAG demo (fictional store).*

## Eligibility
- Gyms, fitness studios, physiotherapy clinics and corporate wellness programs with a registered business.
- Minimum first order: 1,500 EUR. Re-orders: 500 EUR.

## Pricing
- Tier 1 (1,500–4,999 EUR per order): 12% off retail.
- Tier 2 (5,000–14,999 EUR): 18% off retail.
- Tier 3 (15,000 EUR and above): 24% off retail plus a dedicated account manager.
- Prices exclude VAT; EU businesses with a valid VAT ID are invoiced at 0% reverse charge.

## Payment
- First order: 100% prepayment.
- After two completed orders: net-14 invoice terms for Tier 2 and above.

## Lead times
- In-stock items ship within 3 business days.
- Custom-branded equipment (logo on mats, kettlebells, benches) takes 4–6 weeks; a 30% deposit is required.

## Contacts
- Wholesale requests are qualified automatically within minutes; hot leads are called by sales the same day.
