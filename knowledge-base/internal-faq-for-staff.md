# GearUp: Internal FAQ for Staff

*Sample knowledge-base document for the RAG demo (fictional store). Used by the internal assistant, not by customers.*

## Support rules
- Never promise a delivery date earlier than the carrier estimate.
- Refunds above 300 EUR need manager approval before they are confirmed to the customer.
- Angry customers or any mention of a lawyer: hand over to a manager immediately and do not argue.

## Stock and purchasing
- Reorder level per SKU is stored in the Products sheet; when stock is at or below it, purchasing is alerted in Telegram.
- Supplier lead times: fitness accessories 10 days, benches and racks 28 days, electronics 21 days.
- Minimum order quantities are set per supplier; always round the reorder up to the MOQ.

## Working hours
- Support: Monday to Friday 09:00–18:00 CET; the bot answers outside these hours and creates tickets for the morning shift.
- Warehouse dispatch cut-off: 14:00 CET.

## Promo codes
- Only the owner and the marketing lead may create promo codes.
- Maximum discount without owner approval: 20%.
