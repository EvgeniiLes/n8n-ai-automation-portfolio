# GearUp: Shipping & Delivery Policy

*Sample knowledge-base document for the RAG demo (fictional store).*

## Delivery times
- Standard shipping: 3–5 business days within the EU, 7–12 business days to the US and UK.
- Express shipping: 1–2 business days within Germany, 2–4 business days across the EU. Express is not available for orders containing items over 30 kg.
- Orders placed before 14:00 CET on a business day are dispatched the same day if all items are in stock.

## Shipping costs
- Free standard shipping on orders over 75 EUR (EU) or 120 USD (US/UK).
- Standard shipping below the threshold: 6.90 EUR (EU), 14.90 USD (US/UK).
- Express shipping: 12.90 EUR flat.
- Heavy items (barbells, racks, benches over 30 kg) are shipped by freight forwarder: 29 EUR flat, delivery appointment by phone.

## Tracking
- A tracking link is sent by email within 24 hours of dispatch.
- If the tracking number shows no movement for 5 business days, support opens a carrier investigation.

## Lost or damaged parcels
- Report damage within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the box and the item.
- Support ships a replacement or issues a full refund after the carrier claim is opened; the customer does not need to wait for the claim to finish.
