# GearUp: Returns, Refunds & Warranty

*Sample knowledge-base document for the RAG demo (fictional store).*

## Returns
- 30 days from delivery to return any unused item in its original packaging.
- Return shipping is free for wrong size or damaged items; otherwise the customer pays return shipping (6.90 EUR label via our portal).
- Resistance bands, yoga mats and other hygiene items cannot be returned once the seal is broken.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after the warehouse receives the parcel.

## Exchanges
- Free size or colour exchange within 30 days, subject to stock. If the new item is more expensive, the difference is charged; if cheaper, it is refunded.

## Warranty
- 24 months on all electronic equipment (smart scales, heart-rate straps, treadmill motors).
- 12 months on frames, benches and racks, excluding paint wear and normal use marks.
- 6 months on consumables such as grips, straps and mat surfaces.
- Warranty requires the order number; no receipt photo is needed.

## How to start a return
1. Message the support bot with the order number and the email used at checkout.
2. The bot creates a return request and emails a prepaid label (where applicable).
3. A human manager reviews damage or warranty cases within 1 business day.
